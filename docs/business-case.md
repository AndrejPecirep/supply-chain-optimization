# Business Case

## Problem
Manual planning leads to excess stock, stockouts, delayed shipments and weak supplier-risk visibility.

## Value
- Lower working capital through smarter inventory policies.
- Better service levels through 30-90 day demand forecasting.
- Faster supplier-risk detection through AI contract review.
- Lower manual administration by parsing shipping documents.

## KPIs
- Forecast MAPE
- Supplier risk score
- On-time delivery rate
- Inventory turnover
- Stockout incidents
- Expedited freight cost
